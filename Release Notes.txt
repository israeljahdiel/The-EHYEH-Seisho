After learning the original Hebrew name for the Son and the Father, I had this dream:

(In 3rd Grade my house was broken into) The dream started at that location in the house. I tried speaking “Elohim” but I could not close my jaw to form the word. I tried speaking “Adonai” but I could not close my jaw to form the word. Then I went to speak a third time. I was able to close my jaw to form the word “EHYEH,” the name of our Father and Son; [Shemot 3:14]

Twice in other dreams: Speaking YIHOVEH YESHUA HAMASHIACH is what allowed me to turn on the light to escape my dreams, and is also what provided protection against assailants. 

[Yeshayahu 51:15] Another name directly given: YIHOVEH-TZVA’OT

[Yirmeyahu 23:6] Yet Another: YIHOVEH TZIDKENU : Yeshua Hamashiach
